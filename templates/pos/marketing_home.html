Hardware requirements

Use the equipment you already have

POSitive! runs in a modern web browser. No proprietary terminal is required.

Required

Internet-connected device

Windows, macOS, Android, iPadOS, or iOS device with a current Chrome, Edge, or Safari browser and a stable internet connection.

Recommended

Checkout workstation

Desktop, laptop, or tablet with at least a 10-inch display. A physical keyboard helps with large catalogs and reports.

Optional

Retail accessories

USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner, browser-compatible receipt printer, cash drawer, and customer-facing display.

Frequently asked questions

What business owners ask before starting

Can I use POSitive! for a minimart or non-food retail store?

Yes. Product creation includes grocery, fashion, gadget, pet-supply, hardware, general-merchandise, cosmetics, and personal-care categories alongside food-service categories.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The interface adapts to phone, tablet, laptop, and desktop screens. A larger display is recommended for high-volume checkout.

Do I need special hardware?

No proprietary hardware is required. Optional barcode scanners and receipt printers can be added according to your operating workflow.

Can staff share a cash register during the day?

Yes. Opening cash is entered once, staff custody is handed over without resetting the count, and activity can be exported for audit.

How does Pro multi-store access work?

A Pro administrator can use one login across assigned Pro stores, see them in the owner super dashboard, and open each store's workspace without mixing its data.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the active term. Review the published cancellation policy and export important store records before access ends.
