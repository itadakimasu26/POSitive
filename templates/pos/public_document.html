{% if document == 'privacy' %}

1. Scope

This notice applies to visitors, trial users, subscribers, business owners, and staff using POSitive!. The business subscribing to POSitive! controls the customer, product, transaction, and staff records it enters. POSitive! processes those records to provide the service.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and contact details such as names, usernames, email addresses, phone numbers, and business information.
  • Store operations data such as products, inventory movements, sales, receipts, customer records, staff roles, register activity, and reports.
  • Subscription details such as selected plan, payment method, bank reference, status, and proof of payment. POSitive! does not collect or store online card credentials.
  • Security and technical information such as login events, IP address, browser information, and diagnostic logs.
  • Demo requests and messages submitted through the public website.

3. How information is used

We use information to create and secure accounts, run checkout and inventory workflows, generate reports, provide support, verify subscriptions, prevent misuse, diagnose faults, meet legal obligations, and improve the service. We do not sell personal information.

4. Service providers and disclosure

Information may be processed by hosting, managed database, and email-delivery providers that help operate POSitive!, including Vercel, the configured PostgreSQL provider, and Gmail. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the service or its users, or during a legitimate business transfer with appropriate safeguards.

5. Cookies, retention, and choices

Essential session and security cookies keep users signed in and protect forms. Operational data is retained while an account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for support, audit, fraud prevention, and legal needs. Businesses can export key records in CSV format. Requests to access, correct, export, or delete personal information are assessed against ownership, legal, security, and audit requirements.

6. Children and updates

POSitive! is a business service and is not directed to children. We may update this notice as the service or legal requirements change; material changes will be published here with a new effective date.

{% elif document == 'policies' %}

Cancellation policy

You may request cancellation at any time by contacting support from an authorized account email. Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the current paid term, and the business may continue using the active plan until then. Export records you need before access ends.

Refunds

Paid terms are generally non-refundable once activated. We will review duplicate payments, incorrect charges, a service we could not activate, and refunds required by law. Approved refunds use an agreed practical method and may require transaction verification.

Trial and subscription activation

The 30-day trial starts immediately after signup and requires no card. A Landbank payment request is activated after the submitted reference and payment proof are verified. For cash, contact us to arrange and confirm the transaction. Manual payments are not instant and should not be treated as approved until the status changes in POSitive!.

Support hours

Regular local support is available seven days a week, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM Philippine Time, excluding announced service interruptions or exceptional closures. Requests received outside these hours are queued for the next support period. Critical security reports may be sent at any time.

Backup and recovery policy

Production data is stored in the configured managed PostgreSQL service and relies on that provider’s backup and recovery capabilities. POSitive! also supplies CSV exports for important store records and audit workflows. Recovery is handled on a best-effort basis and recovery time or point cannot be guaranteed for every incident. Customers should regularly export records required for their own accounting, tax, and business-continuity needs.

Changes and data access

Plan upgrades take effect after approval and may increase available roles and features. Downgrades or cancellation can limit access after the paid term. Contact support before the end date if you need help exporting business records.

{% elif document == 'security' %}

Security approach

POSitive! uses layered application and hosting controls intended to protect account and store data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so controls are reviewed and improved as the product changes.

Application protections

  • HTTPS encryption is used for traffic to the production site.
  • Passwords are stored using Django’s one-way password hashing, not as readable passwords.
  • CSRF protection, secure session handling, and security-focused cookie settings protect browser requests.
  • Role-based permissions and store membership checks restrict staff to authorized operations and stores.
  • Business records are scoped by store to reduce cross-customer exposure.
  • Production secrets are supplied through protected environment variables rather than committed source files.

Payments and data

Current Landbank and cash subscription workflows record verification information but do not store banking login credentials or online card details. Store transaction and audit records are held in the production database, with access limited through the application and administrative controls.

Monitoring, backups, and response

Application logging and platform diagnostics support fault and security investigation. Recovery relies on the managed database provider plus customer exports as described in the backup policy. Confirmed incidents are contained, investigated, and communicated when notification is appropriate or legally required.

Your responsibilities

Use unique passwords, protect administrator accounts, remove departed staff promptly, assign the minimum necessary role, verify devices and networks used at checkout, and keep exported audit data secure. Do not send passwords or full banking credentials through support messages.

Report a concern

If you believe an account or store has been compromised, contact us immediately with the affected business, approximate time, and a safe callback method. Do not include passwords.
