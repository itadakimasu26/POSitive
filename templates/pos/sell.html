Prepare the offline-sales CSV before an outage

Download a fresh store template at the start of a shift and after prices change. During downtime, copy one catalog row per item sold. Give every order a unique ID such as OFF-20260808-001, use YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM for the time, and repeat its payment method, notes, and tax-inclusive collected total on every line.

  1. Download and keep the template on the checkout device.
  2. Record completed orders while OXPOS is unavailable.
  3. Upload the file after service returns. Validation is all-or-nothing and duplicate order IDs are safely skipped.
